                \begin{itemize}
                    \item (a) Suppose that each square of a $4\times 7$ chessboard, as shown above, is colored either black or white. Prove that with \textit{any} such coloring, the board must contain a rectangle (formed by the horizontal and vertical lines of the board such as the one outlined in the figure) whose four distinct unit corner squares are all of the same color.
\item (b) Exhibit a black-white coloring of a $4\times 6$ board in which the four corner squares of every rectangle, as described above, are not all of the same color.
                \end{itemize}


                

	\item (\textit{USAMO 1976}) If $A$ and $B$ are fixed points on a given circle and $XY$ is a variable diameter of the same circle, determine the locus of the point of intersection of lines $AX$ and $BY$. You may assume that $AB$ is not a diameter.


 
                    \begin{figure}[H]

	\item (\textit{USAMO 1976}) Determine all integral solutions of $a^2+b^2+c^2=a^2b^2$.


 

	\item (\textit{USAMO 1976}) If the sum of the lengths of the six edges of a trirectangular tetrahedron $PABC$ (i.e., $\angle APB=\angle BPC=\angle CPA=90^o$) is $S$, determine its maximum volume.


 

	\item (\textit{USAMO 1976}) If $P(x)$, $Q(x)$, $R(x)$, and $S(x)$ are all \href{https://artofproblemsolving.com/wiki/index.php?title=Polynomial}{polynomials} such that

 \[P(x^5) + xQ(x^5) + x^2 R(x^5) = (x^4 + x^3 + x^2 + x +1) S(x),\]
prove that $x-1$ is a factor of $P(x)$.
